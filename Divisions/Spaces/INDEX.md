# Allternit Spaces

## What it is

Allternit's spaces division: manufactured physical environments — pods, furniture, installations, and rooms — where Allternit compute, robotics, and agents are experienced.

It is the **environment authority**. Spaces are designed and produced by Allternit Manufacturing's seventh operating division; the Spaces division defines the experience, layout, and integration requirements.

## Current phase

R&D.

## What it owns

- Space concepts and experience design
- Installation layouts and integration specs
- Compute/robotics housing requirements
- Public surface: content and visuals for spaces.allternit.com

## What it does not own

- **Physical manufacturing** — that lives in Allternit Manufacturing
- **Compute hardware** — that lives in Allternit Compute
- **Robotics autonomy** — that lives in Allternit Robotics

## Convergence with Manufacturing, Compute, and Robotics

| Spaces receives | Spaces delivers |
|---|---|
| Compute appliances from Compute | Power/cooling/networking requirements for compute housing |
| Robotics platforms from Manufacturing | Placement, charging, and access requirements |
| Manufacturing production capability | Buildable designs, BOMs, and installation guides |
